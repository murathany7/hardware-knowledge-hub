# Code of conduct

This project welcomes people on their first hardware build and on their first open-source contribution. To keep it that way:

- Be kind and patient. Nobody is expected to know a term, a tool or a convention already.
- Criticize the work, not the person, and say what would make it better.
- No harassment, insults, or personal attacks, and no unwelcome attention of any kind.

This applies to issues, pull requests and any other project space. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them.

To report a problem, use [GitHub's report tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) or contact the maintainer privately through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/murathany7).
